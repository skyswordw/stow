HARBOR 3.0 — MOTION NOTES
Round 4, 1 October 2026 · Tomas

The rule: motion explains where something came from. If it doesn't, cut it.

SPRINGS
  Default        response 0.35 s, damping 0.85   sheets, cards, the ticket
  Snappy         response 0.25 s, damping 0.9    toggles, chips, the tab bar
  No bounce on anything that shows money or a departure time.

DEPARTURE BOARD
  New times slide up 8 pt and fade in over 0.2 s.
  A delay doesn't flash. The time changes colour to status.late and stays.
  Cancelled sailings fade to 40% and get a line through them. No shake.

TICKET
  Buying: the ticket rises from the Pay button into the wallet (0.45 s, Default spring).
  At the gate: brightness goes to full and the code pulses once, then holds still.

ONBOARDING
  Photos cross-fade (0.6 s, ease in-out). Text moves 12 pt with the swipe, photos 4 pt.
  The beam in the mark turns once on the last screen. Only there.

REDUCE MOTION
  Every slide becomes a 0.15 s fade. The beam doesn't turn. The board updates in place.

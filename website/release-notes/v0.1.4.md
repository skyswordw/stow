## Stow 0.1.4

This update fixes crashes in Finder menus and window transitions, and makes opening archives and keeping the Tray visible more predictable.

- Fixed a crash when opening a Finder context menu and a crash during extraction/window transitions.
- Archives open directly in the appropriate Browser or Glance window. The last-used window mode is remembered across restarts, with explicit Browser and Glance preferences still available.
- The Tray now hides when you click elsewhere or switch apps, instead of disappearing after an idle timeout. Keep Tray Open prevents automatic hiding; closing and reopening the Tray preserves collected files and an unfinished folder-name draft.
- Clearer Tray pin states and feedback, with matching English and Simplified Chinese explanations.
- More reliable Search focus, clearer keyboard focus indicators, and improved respect for Reduce Motion during keyboard actions.
- Refined archive-opening feedback, folder-access notices and dark-mode surfaces.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
